DSA Lab

Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Last reviewed: November 8, 2026

DSA Lab is an independent educational project. You can use the lab without creating an account. Reports are voluntary: do not send passwords, keys, identity documents, sensitive data, or information about other people.

What stays in your browser

Inputs used to run visualizations are processed in your browser. To remember your preferences between visits, the site uses local storage for language, accessibility settings, selected algorithm, playback speed, code mode, whether you have seen the introduction, and whether the sidebar is collapsed.

Challenge progress, quiz result history, and temporary quiz locks are also stored locally. You can clear these data in your browser settings. This resets preferences and progress saved in that browser but does not delete reports you already sent.

Visits, performance, and security

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to measure visits and performance. Measurements can include the page visited, browser, device type, approximate location, and loading times. We do not use them for personalized advertising. Hosting and security services may process connection details, such as IP addresses, to serve pages, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse.

Report form and verification

Opening the form contacts Railway to check API availability and loads Cloudflare Turnstile verification. These connections may process technical data even if you do not send a report. The field contents are sent to the API when you press “Send report”.

When you send a report, you share the name you enter (an alias is fine), your email if provided, the subject, issue type, description, and any steps you add. The section, page origin and path, and browser information are also sent. The diagnostic URL excludes credentials, query parameters, and fragments.

Sending requires a name or alias of at least two characters, a summary, an issue type, and a description of at least ten characters. Email and reproduction steps are optional. “Copy” places the report text on your device’s clipboard; it does not send the report by itself.

Cloudflare Turnstile uses technical browser and connection signals to detect automated submissions. The API on Railway uses the IP address to limit abuse and sends it to Cloudflare when verifying the security token. Resend delivers the report to the DSA Lab inbox. An optional email address is used to reply to your report, not to subscribe you to a mailing list.

Access, use, and retention

We do not publish reports or sell the data you submit. We use it to investigate the issue, reply if you provided an email address, and protect the service. The providers named above perform those functions and may process data outside your country.

The API does not maintain a report database. Report emails are deleted from the inbox no later than 90 days after the case is closed, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. Inbox deletion is manual. You can request earlier deletion through the channel below. Copies and technical logs held by providers have their own retention periods.

A case is closed when it is resolved or a decision is made to end its handling. Open cases are reviewed periodically so that data no longer needed are not retained. Inbox deletion does not mean that providers’ backup copies are immediately erased.

Privacy questions and requests

To ask about your data or request access, correction, deletion, or another applicable right, return to DSA Lab, open “Report a problem”, select “Another problem”, and use “Privacy request” as the summary.

Explain what you need and provide an email address for a reply. If your request relates to an earlier report, include its subject and approximate date if you remember them. We may need to verify that the data are yours before sharing or changing them. Do not attach identity documents or repeat sensitive information in the form.
